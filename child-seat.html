Child Seat Guide for Family Travel

When travelling with children, let our team know your child’s age and seat requirements when you arrange your journey. This guide explains the toddler and booster seat types shown for My London Transfer journeys, and the measurements commonly used to describe them.

Request a child seat
01 · TODDLER SEAT

Toddler Seat

A forward-facing seat category for a growing toddler. Tell the booking team your child’s age, height and weight so they can confirm the suitable arrangement for your trip.

Weight
9–18 kg / 20–40 lbs
Age
Approximately 15 months–4 years
Height
76–105 cm / 2′6″–3′5″
Group
Group 1

What to share when requesting one

  • Your child’s current age and approximate height.
  • Approximate weight if known.
  • Whether you need a seat for the outbound ride, return ride, or both.
  • Any relevant fitting or comfort requirements.

Seat availability and suitability should be confirmed with the transfer team before travel. Please include your request when making the booking so the requirements can be checked in advance.

02 · BOOSTER SEAT

Booster Seat

A booster helps raise a child into a position intended for the vehicle’s adult seat belt. Share your child’s measurements when you contact the team to request the right option.

Weight
15–36 kg / 33–80 lbs
Age
Approximately 3.5–12 years
Height
100–135 cm / 3′3″–4′5″
Group
Groups 2/3

Before your journey

  • Request the booster when you make your transfer booking.
  • Give the team your child’s age, height and weight range.
  • Allow the team to confirm seat availability for each leg of the journey.
  • Check the fit and belt position with the driver before setting off.

Children grow at different rates, so age alone may not determine the appropriate seat. The listed specifications are a practical guide; confirm your child’s needs with the booking team.

Seat requests and safety

My London Transfer asks customers to request child seats as part of their booking. Confirm availability and suitability directly with the team before your journey. The descriptions above are general category guidance and are not a substitute for checking your child’s individual needs or the seat manufacturer’s instructions.

Child Seat Questions

How do I request a child seat?

Include your requirement with your transfer request and provide your child’s age, height and weight. Confirm that the seat is available before travel.

What should I tell the booking team?

Share the child’s approximate age, height and weight, plus which journeys need a seat.

Are infant seats shown in this guide?

This page covers the toddler and booster seat types shown in the current guide. Contact My London Transfer directly about any other seating requirements.

Can I request a seat for a return journey?

Yes. Mention both legs when arranging your journey and ask the team to confirm the request for each one.
